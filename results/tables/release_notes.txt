Public release notes

This repository contains the analysis code, aggregate analytical outputs, publication figures and supplementary tables accompanying the revised manuscript.

The primary analysis evaluates a fixed GradientBoostingClassifier using repeated nested cross-validation. Supplementary materials distinguish the fixed primary analysis from post-review auxiliary analyses, including an inner-tuned GBM comparison and sensitivity analyses for missingness, centre and preprocessing choices. These auxiliary analyses do not replace the primary model and are reported with their corresponding limitations.

To prevent duplicate hosting of patient-level clinical records, original clinical predictors, derived raw clinical scores, and ground-truth 90-day mRS outcome labels are not redistributed. Cross-validation fold assignments and label-free model predicted probabilities are provided in outputs/ to support independent metric verification. Trained model checkpoint binaries are not included. The public source ISLES 2024 data release is identified in data/README.md.

For detailed methods, estimates and limitations, see S1_Text_analysis_details.txt and the accompanying manuscript.
